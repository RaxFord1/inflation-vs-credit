Macro & horizon

Numbers behind the result

Model notes: annuity schedule with nominal-rate/12 monthly interest; investment interest compounds monthly and is taxed as it accrues; insurance and maintenance are recalculated on the asset's current value; the asset appears in both net-worth lines where both sides hold it. "Today's ₴/$" deflate by the respective CPI. On the life-decisions tab, every path earns the same salary and pays the same living expenses; income changes (new job, education, business, moving) are modelled as differences from your current salary; a plan whose account dips below zero is flagged as not fitting the budget (past that point the numbers assume borrowing at the investment rate, which flatters it). The decision score is a weighted mix of five 0–100 criteria: wealth and crisis-wealth are scaled between the worst and best path on screen (so scores are relative, not absolute), payment stress uses the peak share of salary, liquidity is the share of final wealth held in the investment account rather than in property, cars or offset by debt, and lifestyle is your own rating. Not modelled: devaluation shocks (real UAH moves stepwise, not smoothly), rate resets on the loan, early repayment, deposit-guarantee and bank risk, taxes on selling property, and the option value of holding liquid cash.
